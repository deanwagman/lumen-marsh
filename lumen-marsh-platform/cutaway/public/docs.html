Opening

Now holds the cue and the one current action. It follows Operator knows and Guest sees. Lightning hold is the action in Now only while the park is quiet. Cypress Coil vibration and Mangrove disruption stay under Explore other scenarios. Clear the storm moves into Now only after guest guidance is published and every affected attraction is observably on weather hold.

There is no Next button. Now does not publish. Publishing stays inside Control Tower.

  1. Quiet park. The cue says press Lightning hold. That button is marked Start here. The line under it says Control Tower will get a decision, and the guest room will not, until someone publishes.
  2. Recommendation received. Pending weather shows under Operator knows. In Control Tower, review or acknowledge the recommendation. That review does not put a ride on hold.
  3. Guest still on the last picture. The cue highlights the guest room and Guest sees. It says: the ride still looks open. That is the published picture. The advisory still reads Quiet, and the projection still reads Unchanged.
  4. Hold, publish, then clear. In Control Tower, place each affected attraction on weather hold as a separate attraction command. Publish guest guidance after the operational decision. The hosted room opens with the temporary authority for your demo turn; no token or sign-in is shown in Cutaway. When the guidance is published and all affected attractions are held, the cue says press Clear the storm.
  5. Return-to-service review. Clear the storm creates a clearance recommendation; it does not clear a ride or withdraw guidance. Now points to Control Tower and names the published guidance and held rides it can still observe. Review the recommendation, clear each weather hold, complete testing, and approve return to service when appropriate. Withdraw guest guidance separately.

An active hold recommendation with quiet guest guidance is one reading. The page walks through review, the unchanged guest picture, the separate attraction hold, and publication. Control Tower provides a typed weather action so Cutaway can distinguish Place weather hold from Return to service even though both recommendations are Active.

Acknowledging the first recommendation removes its pending card, but does not hold a ride. Control Tower keeps an authenticated active-workflow reading with the typed action and affected attraction IDs, so a new browser still asks for the separate ride holds. Browser-tab memory only bridges the brief trigger and polling race. Start here returns only after the handled return workflow has no pending weather action, guest guidance is quiet, every affected attraction is operating, and the related incident no longer appears open. The monitor does not make operator decisions, and Cutaway never uses its full reset endpoint as a shortcut.

Under the comparison, one line stays with Operator knows and Guest sees. The strip is the seam. Pending on one side, published on the other.

What Cutaway is

Cutaway is a guided exhibit. The rooms are the center.

  • Now: the current cue, and one action. On a quiet park that action is Lightning hold.
  • Guest and Control Tower, side by side on a wide screen. On a narrow screen, the Guest and Control Tower tabs. Each room stays wide enough to read.
  • Operator knows and Guest sees, directly under the rooms.
  • Explore other scenarios: vibration, queue disruption, and any trigger that is not the current action.

Now only starts a sensor situation. Reviewing a recommendation, holding a ride, clearing its hold, completing testing, approving return to service, and publishing or withdrawing what guests read all stay in Control Tower.

Lightning hold, Cypress Coil vibration, and Mangrove disruption leave the guest on Today and bring the matching card into view in Control Tower. Clear the storm leaves both rooms where they are.

If the core booth services are unavailable, one message covers both rooms and the actions stay off. If one producer is unavailable, only its matching scenario is disabled; both rooms and the other scenarios remain usable.

How to open it

You are in Cutaway. Choose Cutaway in the header to see both rooms. Choose Docs to come back to this page.

The guest room opens on Today. Control Tower opens ready for the operator. Now starts at Lightning hold. The operator’s clicks stay inside Control Tower.

Turns and Start over

Cutaway takes a short turn automatically when the demo is free. The bar at the top shows your remaining time and a compact readiness check. Choose End turn when you are finished. If another visitor has the turn, you are a spectator: both rooms remain visible, but scenario and reset controls wait until the demo is available.

Start over is available only during your turn. It asks for confirmation because the park is shared, then reports reset, room reload, and quiet-opening verification progress. If reset or verification fails, use Retry. A successful reset clears this tab’s walkthrough state and reloads both rooms.

Explore other scenarios

The current action sits in Now, with Ready, Fired, or Failed beside it. The others stay collapsed under Explore other scenarios until you open that list. Each button shows Fired when it starts, or Failed when it does not.

  • Lightning hold. Starts the lightning situation. The strip shows pending weather, and Control Tower brings that weather card into view. The guest stays on Today, with no advisory yet.
  • Clear the storm. Creates a return-to-service recommendation and leaves published guidance in place. Both rooms stay where they are. Review, ride clearance, testing, return-to-service approval, and guidance withdrawal still happen in Control Tower.
  • Cypress Coil vibration. Starts a vibration cue for Cypress Coil. The strip shows pending reliability, and Control Tower brings that card into view. The guest stays on Today and does not hear about the work.
  • Mangrove disruption. Starts a queue cue for Mangrove Run. The strip shows flow that has not been published, and Control Tower brings that card into view. The guest stays on Today. Best Next does not treat Mangrove Run as down.

If Mangrove disruption has already been used, the strip points to Start over instead of a new card.

What the operator still clicks

Now never holds a ride, accepts a recommendation, returns a ride to service, or publishes or withdraws what guests read. Those clicks are in Control Tower.

  • After lightning, review or acknowledge the weather card and create or link the incident. Then open each affected attraction, choose Place weather hold, enter the reason, and confirm the command. Publishing guest guidance is a separate incident action. Until publication, Guest sees keeps the advisory Quiet and the projection Unchanged.
  • After vibration, accept the reliability recommendation and carry the work through inspection and testing. The cue does not open that work by itself. Guests do not see work-order numbers, asset codes, or internal notes.
  • After Mangrove disruption, approve the recommendation if you want to use it, then publish it as a supervisor. Best Next stays empty until that publish.
  • After Clear the storm, review the return-to-service recommendation. For each held attraction, clear the weather hold, complete testing, and approve return to service when appropriate. Withdraw published guest guidance separately. Cutaway returns to the quiet opening only after the pending weather action, guidance, and observable weather holds are gone.

What the guest must not see until publish

Nothing on the guest side changes until a supervisor publishes it.

  • Today does not gain a park advisory from lightning. The pending weather card is for the operator.
  • Vibration does not show reliability notes to the guest. The guest projection stays unchanged.
  • Best Next does not mark Mangrove Run down, and does not show the unpublished message.

Operator knows and Guest sees are that split. Operator knows can show a pending card while Guest sees stays quiet. Quiet means it has not been published. The strip is the seam. Pending on one side, published on the other.
