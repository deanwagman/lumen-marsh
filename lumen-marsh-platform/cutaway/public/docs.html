Opening

The cue on the rail is the marked path. It follows the strip. Lightning hold is the only button it marks. Cypress Coil vibration and Mangrove disruption stay unmarked. Clear the storm stays unmarked until a guest advisory is on the strip.

There is no Next button. The rail does not publish. Publishing stays inside Control Tower.

  1. Quiet park. The cue says press Lightning hold. That button is marked Start here. The line under it says Control Tower will get a decision, and the guest room will not, until someone publishes.
  2. Hold accepted. Pending weather shows on the operator side of the strip. The cue highlights Control Tower and that side. It says: look right. That row is the ask. Leave it unpublished, then look left.
  3. Guest still on the last picture. The cue highlights the guest room and the guest side of the strip. It says: the ride still looks open. That is the published picture. The advisory still reads Quiet, and the projection still reads Unchanged.
  4. Publish, then clear. The cue names the action inside Control Tower: review the weather recommendation and publish the guest advisory. This room opens as Local Operator, already signed in. If it shows VenueOps sign in, sign in with your operator account to open the console. When the strip’s guest advisory changes, the cue says press Clear the storm.

Pending weather with a quiet guest advisory is one reading. The strip cannot tell the Control Tower look, the guest look, and the publish line apart. The page shows them in that order, one room at a time. The change the strip can see is a guest advisory that is no longer Quiet. That is when the cue says press Clear the storm.

Open the page during a hold and the cue starts from the strip, not from Start here. Clear the storm is the way back to the quiet park. Start here returns when pending weather is none and the guest advisory is Quiet again. Pressing Clear the storm does not publish, and it does not by itself take a guest advisory off the strip.

One line stays under the cue. The strip is the seam. Pending on one side, published on the other.

What Cutaway is

Cutaway opens two rooms at the same time.

  • Left: the guest, on Today. Best Next lives there.
  • Right: Control Tower, where the operator works.
  • The rail, outside both rooms: the opening cue, and four triggers. Lightning hold is the marked path.
  • The strip underneath: the latest recommendation, and whether the guest’s view has changed.

The rail only starts a situation. Holding a ride, accepting a recommendation, dismissing one, and publishing what guests read all stay in Control Tower.

Lightning hold, Cypress Coil vibration, and Mangrove disruption leave the guest on Today and bring the matching card into view in Control Tower. Clear the storm leaves both rooms where they are.

If the booth cannot reach the park, both rooms are covered and the rail stays off until it can.

How to open it

You are in Cutaway. Choose Cutaway in the header to see both rooms. Choose Docs to come back to this page.

The guest room opens on Today. Control Tower opens ready for the operator. The opening cue starts at Lightning hold. The operator’s clicks stay inside Control Tower.

What each rail button does

Each button shows Fired when the cue starts, or Failed when it does not.

  • Lightning hold. Starts the lightning situation. The strip shows pending weather, and Control Tower brings that weather card into view. The guest stays on Today, with no advisory yet.
  • Clear the storm. Moves the weather into the period after the hold. Both rooms stay where they are. Sending rides back into service still happens in Control Tower.
  • Cypress Coil vibration. Starts a vibration cue for Cypress Coil. The strip shows pending reliability, and Control Tower brings that card into view. The guest stays on Today and does not hear about the work.
  • Mangrove disruption. Starts a queue cue for Mangrove Run. The strip shows flow that has not been published, and Control Tower brings that card into view. The guest stays on Today. Best Next does not treat Mangrove Run as down.

If Mangrove disruption has already been used, the strip shows a note instead of a new card. That note is not a button.

What the operator still clicks

The rail never holds a ride, accepts a recommendation, dismisses one, or publishes what guests read. Those clicks are in Control Tower.

  • After lightning, review the weather card, open the incident, and place the hold. Publish the guest advisory when Today should show it. Until then, the strip keeps the guest advisory Quiet and the projection Unchanged.
  • After vibration, accept the reliability recommendation and carry the work through inspection and testing. The cue does not open that work by itself. Guests do not see work-order numbers, asset codes, or internal notes.
  • After Mangrove disruption, approve the recommendation if you want to use it, then publish it as a supervisor. Best Next stays empty until that publish.
  • After clearing the storm, return to service is still a click in Control Tower.

What the guest must not see until publish

Nothing on the guest side changes until a supervisor publishes it.

  • Today does not gain a park advisory from lightning. The pending weather card is for the operator.
  • Vibration does not show reliability notes to the guest. The guest projection stays unchanged.
  • Best Next does not mark Mangrove Run down, and does not show the unpublished message.

The strip is how you see that split. The operator side can show a pending card while the guest side stays quiet. Quiet means it has not been published.
